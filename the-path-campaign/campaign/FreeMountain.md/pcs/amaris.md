# Amaris

**Catfolk Witch** — FreeMountain campaign

## Background

Amaris was born under a blood moon, during a meteor shower. Her tribe watches the stars, a habit older than anyone remembers, so a sky like that marked her as someone special from her first night.

Growing up, she heard the stories everyone hears: werewolves, vampires, demigods. One of them was told more quietly than the rest. Long ago, an ancient vampire lived with the tribe. He brought them wealth and power. Then one day he said he was going to perform a ritual and took half the tribe with him into the mist. None of them came back. The story was never written down. The tribe split that day, and nobody likes to talk about it.

It was the tribe's shaman who told her the rest. After the others vanished, some people saw a meteor shower, and they said it was the souls of the lost drifting up to heaven together. It was the same sky Amaris was born under.

She couldn't let it go. She left the tribe and went out into the world, searching old books and old maps for any record of that shower. Finally she found one: long ago, that shower had been seen hanging over the mist. That was where they had gone, so that was where she went.

She went into the mist, and now she can't find her way out. Since she's stuck anyway, she may as well find out what happened to the Ancestor.

## What she believes

- An ancient vampire lived with her tribe, long ago. The legends describe what he looked like and little else.
- He left with half the tribe to perform a ritual. None of them returned.
- The meteor shower seen afterwards was the souls of the lost rising together.
- The shower stood over the mist — so the ritual site is somewhere in the mist.

## GM: what really happened

See [the-ancestor.yaml](../../../lore/factions/factionless/npcs/the-ancestor.yaml).

- **He was never one of them.** He came to the tribe as an outsider, already made a vampire by the Fate rune. The tribe was his herd. "The Ancestor" is a name of worship, not of blood — Amaris is not his descendant.
- **The tribe's star-lore is his.** They copied his research into the sky and kept it as ritual without knowing what he was looking for. Her "special" birth is special by a standard no one in the tribe understands.
- **The ritual was a slaughter.** He led the half of the tribe to the blood stone beneath the Great Mangrove in The Reeds and killed them on it. Their blood made the Sacrifice rune (Fehu) and is still in its grooves. Croa draws on it every morning without knowing whose it is.
- **The souls never rose.** The meteor shower was his ascension in the Eisenwald, where seven of his inner circle gave themselves willingly. The mourners' comfort is half true: people did die under that shower, but not the ones they were mourning. Those had already died in the swamp.
- **The seven were her people.** The ritual turned them into werecats. They have bred since, and the Eisenwald is full of their feral descendants. The beasts hunting her there are what became of her tribe.
- **The sky is still there.** The Eisenwald's sky is frozen at the moment of the ascension. When Amaris reaches the iron forest, she will see the sky she was born under, held in place overhead.

## Threads

- **The Eisenwald** — where her research points. The recognition of the frozen sky is her scene.
- **The Reeds** — the stop her legend skipped. She can stand where her people died without knowing it.
- **The shaman** — keeper of the secret version of the legend; her tie back to the tribe.
- **The Ancestor's path** — open to her as to any PC (the Fate rune, the ascension ritual). She does no hemomancy and is not drawn to it. Do not steer her toward or away from it.

## Notes

- Her birth sky echoing the ascension sky is a nod, not a mechanic. It grants no power and no destiny.
- The "great cosmic event approaching" on her character sheet is set aside for now.
