Open Skies Reading Toggle planets and runes below

Available wherever the GM rules the sky is open above the mist. Once per rest, a player may attempt an Instinct roll (or argue another stat) to read tonight's sky. The GM sets which planet is prominent — its domain determines what kind of question the stars can answer.

A character carrying the Finding rune stone can also read the rune standing among the stars. The GM sets one rune — it shapes what kind of answer the reading gives.
